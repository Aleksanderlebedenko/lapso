I bought Premium and it is not active

Open Lapso, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription or a free trial?

On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lapso › Cancel. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple, not by the developer. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you live in the European Union, you may also have the right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days; Apple handles that on the same page.

What happens to my data if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted. The timer, two themes and six months of statistics stay free. Sessions older than six months are kept, hidden from the statistics, and appear again if Premium returns.

The timer did not make a sound

Check that notifications are allowed for Lapso in Settings › Notifications › Lapso, that a Focus mode is not silencing them, and that the sound in Lapso › Settings › Sounds is not set to None. In silent mode the iPhone vibrates instead of playing the sound.

A session I stopped early is missing

Only finished sessions are counted. A session that is cancelled before the end is not saved.
