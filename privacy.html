Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Lapso does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

Who is responsible

Lapso is made by Oleksandr Lebedenko, an independent developer based in Spain. For any question about privacy, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE.

What the app stores, and where

On your iPhone. Your focus sessions (when they started, how long they lasted and which theme they belong to), your themes and your settings are saved in files inside the app on your device. The developer cannot see them.

In your own iCloud, if sync is on. When iCloud sync is available and iCloud is switched on for Lapso, the same data is copied to the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, so that your other devices can show it. That database belongs to you. Apple stores it, and the developer has no access to it. You can switch this off in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Nowhere else. The app does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. It has no servers of its own. The files are part of your own device backups, if you use them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or to give your name, email address or phone number.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not contain analytics, tracking or any other third-party code. It is built only with Apple's own frameworks.
  • It does not track you across apps and websites.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the developer never receives your card details or your Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple, on your device, whether Premium is active. Apple gives the developer sales reports with totals per country, which do not identify you. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lapso uses them to tell you that a session or a break has ended, and to remind you two days before a free trial ends. These notifications are created on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Information Apple may share with the developer

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer combined statistics, such as the number of installs or crash reports. They do not identify you, and you can switch this off there at any time.

When you write to support

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write. It is used only to answer you, on the legal basis of the legitimate interest in replying to your request, and is deleted within 24 months of the last message. The mailbox is run by Google (Gmail), which processes the messages on the developer's behalf and may do so in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub records technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, in its server logs, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. GitHub is a company in the United States and takes part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. It keeps these logs to run and protect its service, and the developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. If iCloud sync was on, you can also delete the copy in iCloud: Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage › Lapso › Delete Data from iCloud.

Children

Lapso can be used at any age and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, and for its correction, deletion, restriction or transfer, and the right to object. Because the developer holds no data from the app, these rights apply in practice to support emails. Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts to collect data, the policy and the app's privacy label in the App Store will say so before that version is released.
