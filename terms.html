What is free and what is Premium

Free, with no time limit:

  • The full timer, with focus sessions, breaks, sounds and notifications
  • Two themes of your choice
  • Statistics for the last six months
  • Lock screen controls

Premium adds:

  • All five themes, plus custom themes of your own
  • Any duration from 1 to 180 minutes, and separate durations for each theme
  • Your full history

Premium is offered as a yearly subscription, a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices depend on your country and are shown in the App Store before you confirm. All purchases go through Apple.

Subscriptions

Payment. The price is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends.

Automatic renewal. A subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.

Managing and cancelling. On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. There you can see the renewal date, change the plan or cancel. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Free trial. The yearly plan starts with a 14-day free trial, if your Apple Account is eligible for it. When the trial ends, it turns into the paid yearly plan unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial. If you allow notifications, Lapso sends you a reminder on your iPhone two days before the trial ends.

Lifetime purchase

The lifetime purchase is a single payment. It does not renew, and it is never charged again. It unlocks Premium for the Apple Account that bought it. On another device, open Lapso, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.
